AI powered travel website

This project explores an AI-powered travel website that helps people discover meaningful local experiences based on their interests, preferences, budget, location and available time.

Tools

Prototype

Problem

  • Not enough personalisation or genuine local experiences from basic recommendations
  • Not enough time and knowledge to plan a trip
  • Pressure to do extensive research from unreliable reviews for the most authentic experiences

Solution

  • Using AI recommendation based on your own personal preferences for best results
  • Fast, flexible and reliable itineraries
  • Getting all necesssary information in one place with just a few clicks

Pain points

  • Too much information and generic results
  • Fixed, non personalised itineraries
  • Difficulty with checking reviews credibility from different local sources

Benefits

  • Easier and faster research, planning and booking processes
  • Storing all needed information in one place
  • Personalized insights and authentic experiences

Competitor analysis

    visual AI planning

  • Triply →

Key findings

AI travel websites focus on producing either fast itinerary or a complicated process for personalisation.

Users don't simply want to copy a popular plan without making personal customization

Experiencing an authentic local adventure instead of going to another tourist trap is one the most important services we can offer.

Users need clean and easy design, short and low effort experience instead of long physical or digital interactions.

Final outcome

Travel planning is often overwhelming. People have to search across multiple platforms to find places, activities, restaurants, and hidden gems that actually match their interests. Itinera is a travel platform that combines AI personalization with local knowledge, helping people spend less time planning and more time experiencing the destination all from one website and with one click.

The website gives users:

  • Flexibility in choosing their preferred way of travel, weather, adventures and experiences.
  • Realistic local recommendations based on a wide internal review system.
  • Offline map and small dictionary with added emergency numbers so you are prepared for every situation.
  • Ready but editable packing lists based on the number and type of users.
  • One stop for all important travel and reservation numbers from any platform
