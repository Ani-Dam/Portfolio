Language learning app

Jaziko is a language learning app focused on teaching the language in its absolute form while staying flexible to individual needs, especially for correct pronunciation and passive learning.

Prototype

Problem

  • Traditional language apps can feel repetitive, basic and boring
  • Users rely on recognition patterns and memorising vocabulary instead of understanding and using the language

Solution

  • Pronunciation exercises + immediate feedbacknd
  • Contextual grammar exercises
  • Listening exercises using natural speech
  • Combine listening, pronunciation and grammar around the same content

Pain points

  • Difficulty understanding native speakers
  • Grammar feels theoretical
  • Lack of motivation
  • Repetitive and boring exercises

Benefits

  • Improves real-world comprehension
  • Creates a more cohesive learning experience
  • Performance feedback and personalized recommendations make practice more targeted

Competitor analysis

Duolingo offers speaking-focused practice and prioritizes engagement over depth
Babbel combines pronunciation, listening, speaking and grammar with speech recognition but is less playful or engaging
Busuu covers the four major skills and has speaking practice with immediate feedback although it uses a broad rather than deeply specialized approach
ELSA is particularly focused on pronunciation and speaking

Key findings

Most competitors cover everything, but don't necessarily specialize in everything

There is a gap between 'getting the answer right' and 'being able to communicate'

The real challenge is connecting all the segments into a useful everyday skill

Final outcome

A focused language-learning experience that connects pronunciation, listening, and grammar in one continuous learning journey. The result was a language-learning experience designed around active language use rather than passive memorization.

The app gives learners:

  • Contextual grammar exercises rather than isolated rules.
  • Realistic listening practice to bridge the gap between textbook audio and natural speech.
  • Pronunciation practice with immediate, actionable feedback.
  • Connected exercises, so the same sentence or concept can be heard, understood, and spoken.
  • Progress and performance insights so learners know which areas need more practice.
  • A more focused experience than general language-learning apps, while avoiding the narrow focus of pronunciation-only products.
