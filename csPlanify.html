Habit tracking app

Building healthier routines through reflection,consistency and better sleep.This project explores a habit-tracking app that combines habit tracking and making,journaling,sleep tracking,to-do lists and money trackering into one experience.

Timeline

May 2026

Role

Ux\Ui Research,
Ux\Ui Design,
User persona,
User flows,
Wireframing,
Prototyping

Tools

Figma

Prototype

Problem

  • Separate apps for every feature
  • People often want to build healthier routines but struggle to maintain them consistently
  • Pressure to maintain a perfect streak without making an impact in everyday life

Solution

  • One app for every need
  • Keeping the streak pressure off by providing a challenge yourself option
  • Building recommendations based on the actual user and their interests and weaknesses

Pain points

  • Lack of consistency, motivation and context
  • Perfect streaks result in preassure
  • Keeping up with every task without meaningfull insights and recommendation

Benefits

  • Easier daily tracking and better awareness
  • More meaningful progress feedback
  • Personalized behavioral insights
  • Greater understanding of their routines

Competitor analysis

  • Habitica
    Strongest point for gamifying daily tasks
  • Streaks
    Focuses on building habits by limiting the overwhelming options
  • Day One
    Building a journaling habbit with mutimedia entry and metadata tracking
  • Sleep Cycle
    Focuses primarily on sleeping data and optimisation of sleep cycles

Key findings

Habit apps are often focused on completion while journals and sleep trackers are just a filler option

Users don't simply want to track their behavior, they want to understand it

Keeping a sense of customability will allow the user to hit their goals and build new habbits more succesfully

Final outcome

The final product creates a more meaningful approach to habit building by connecting daily behaviors, emotions, and sleep. Instead of focusing solely on streaks and completion rates, the experience helps users recognize patterns, understand what influences their routines, and make more informed decisions about their daily habits.

The app gives users:

  • Flexibility in choosing their preferred way of engagement
  • Realistic recommendations based on their preferences and overall use of the app.
  • Journaling and sleep tracking segments do not feel like a chore but an addition.
  • The ability to use the to-do lists and money tracking as completely individual and personalised segments.
  • Opportunity for the most competitive users to communicate and show off in front of their personal network of users or for the more laidback users to just acknowledge their day to day errands.
