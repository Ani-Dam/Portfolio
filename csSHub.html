Website for all local services

Focusing on a defined region, ServiceHub gives you everything you may need - fast research process, transparent prices, real reviews and availabillity for the customer, as well as a one stop shop for professionals to advertise their services in the community.

Prototype

Problem

  • Local customers need reliable services, but finding the right professional is often slow, uncertain and fragmented
  • Local service providers struggle to reach new customers fast and in one place

Solution

  • A two-sided local marketplace that allows customers to discover, compare and book local services, while giving professionals a platform to advertise their services, showcase their work, and receive new job opportunities

Pain points

  • Hard to find trustworthy local professionals fast with clear pricing, reviews and availability
  • Services have difficulty showing their skills, reviews, prices and availability in one place

Benefits

  • Customers save time, energy and gain confidence when booking a service
  • Service providers gain visibility, more bookings and a simple way to grow their local business.

Competitor analysis

  • Taskrabbit
    Book local tasks with a good end-to-end booking experience
  • Thumbtack
    Request quotes/leads from a wide range of local services
  • Bark
    Offers very broad service categories
  • Angi
    Home-service marketplace with established local providers

Key findings

Customers need a web site that makes their life easier with offers for every kind of service in their region and a fast process for booking it, while having all necessary information - pricing, reviews and ratings.

Businesses need to offer their services to a wide net of people, have the process be fast and transparent so they can book all of their availability slots without spending unnecessary time and effort.

Final outcome

Allowing the community to lead the popularity, reliability and pricing of local services by having a website with transparency and wide operational coverage helps the customers and the businesses to maintain a delicate balance.By offering the customers to find every answer to their need on one website, we are making their decision process faster, more reliable and customised to their actual needs.

On the other side we offer a large space for all of the smaller local businesses to communicate and advertise to larger crowd they normally would not have access to.

The website gives users:

  • All necessary information when looking for a certain service - price, location, reliability and availability
  • Opportunity to simplify everyday tasks by booking professionals
  • A live community for spotting reliable brands, trends in certain professions and outstanding service
  • Digital marketing space for every business that has a certain service in a defined area
